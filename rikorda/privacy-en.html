Rikorda privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short

Rikorda does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The subscriptions you enter stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud account.

Data controller

Opem Gestionali di Mazzuferi Sara
Email: info@opemgestionali.com

What data the app handles

Only what you enter: names, amounts, dates and notes of your subscriptions, categories, exchange rates and settings. The name you give at first launch (optional) is only used to greet you and stays on this device. All calculations happen on device; the app doesn't connect to any server of ours or of third parties and doesn't use analytics services.

iCloud

If iCloud is on, the app's data (except your name) is stored in the private database of your iCloud account, so you find it on all your devices with the same Apple ID. Syncing is handled by Apple according to your settings and the iCloud terms; we have no access to this data.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled and delivered by your device: they don't go through any server. The app asks for permission only when you create your first reminder; you can deny or revoke it in Settings and the app keeps working. Notifications include the subscription's name, amount and date: you can hide previews on the Lock Screen in Settings › Notifications.

Backup and export

A backup file (JSON or CSV) leaves your device only when you save or share it, to the location or service you choose. From then on, that service's rules apply. If iCloud Backup or computer backup is on, the app's data may be included in your device backup, managed by Apple according to your settings.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device (and in iCloud, if on) until you delete it in the app; deleting it on one device removes it from the others too. Deleting the app deletes the data on the device, while data in iCloud remains until you delete it in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from any user, including children.

Your rights

Since we don't collect personal data, we have no data to access, correct or delete on your behalf. For any question you can write to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with the new date.
