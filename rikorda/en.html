Frequently asked questions

I don't get reminders.
Check that notifications for Rikorda are allowed in Settings › Notifications › Rikorda and, in the app, in Settings › Reminders. If general notifications are off, only the custom reminders of a single subscription arrive. A Focus may silence notifications. Open the app now and then: iOS allows up to 64 scheduled reminders at a time and the app adds new ones when you open it.

How do I see my subscriptions on my iPad (or a new iPhone)?
Use the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on: your data syncs automatically. Without iCloud you can move it with Settings › Backup › Export and then Import a backup on the other device.

How is past spending calculated?
It's an estimate: the app assumes every charge happened from the first charge on, following the cycle and prices you entered, excluding free trials, pauses and the time after cancellation. If the price changed over time, add the changes to the subscription.

How do other currencies work?
Each subscription has its own currency. Totals are in your base currency, using the rates you set in Settings › Exchange rates: the app never downloads rates from the internet. One rate per currency applies to every period. If a rate is missing, the amount is shown separately with a warning.

Can I change a subscription's currency?
No: the currency is chosen when you create it. To use another one, cancel the subscription (keeping its past spending) and create a new one.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Rikorda doesn't send data to any server; a connection is only used by iCloud to sync your data across your devices.
